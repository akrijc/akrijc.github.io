Global Logistics Empire

I am an amateur developer and this is the first game I am working on. Global Logistics Empire is a manager-tycoon game where you start from almost nothing — with just a small van — and work your way up to building a massive logistics empire.

Build Your Company

Expand your business by constructing a wide variety of buildings that improve your efficiency, increase your capacity, and unlock further progression. Buildings are divided into four categories: Economic, Logistics, Ecological, and Service. Each category opens up new possibilities and helps you grow your empire faster.

Research & Technology

Research plays a key role in your progress. Unlock upgrades and new capabilities across five research branches: Efficiency, Transport Speed, Automation, Safety, and Ecology. The deeper you research, the more powerful your company becomes.

Four Modes of Transport

You start with road transport only. As you progress, you gradually unlock three additional transport modes: rail, sea, and air. Build and manage a multi-modal transport network that spans the entire map.

Wide Range of Cargo

Transport a huge variety of goods including electronics, consumer products, bulk materials, liquid cargo, hazardous materials, and high-value shipments. Each cargo type brings its own challenges and opportunities.
